Roguelite Run Guide

Sanctum Field Guide

A field guide to protecting the Altar. This is the live rules reference; the optional Play Tutorial walks through the controls on a temporary board.

The Sanctum & Your Priest

15 Floors • 3 Acts. Bosses guard Floors 5, 10, and 15. Clear passages to choose rewards and routes. Priest hands, the Altar, Demon Blood, corruption, Relics, and your run deck persist between passages.
Priest cards merge into Empowered cards. Combine two matching unempowered cards from your hand into one stronger version. The merge itself is free, but the Empowered card uses its own Mana cost and effect when played. Drafted rewards add more copies to your run deck.
Basic Attack & Rituals. Each Priest gets a free Basic Attack during their turn. Rituals spend Demon Blood, not Mana. Read the Lesser and Greater rolls before using a Ritual; the descriptions reflect your current Relic Power.

Wards & Laws of Damnation

Wards — room defenses. After every third combat passage, choose one of three shared Wards. During a Priest turn use Place Ward and select an eligible room, spending the Ward’s displayed shared Mana and sometimes Demon Blood. Only one Ward can be active at once. Its charges trigger when Demons enter, leave, or are defeated in that room; unused Wards expire after two Demon Phases or when the passage ends. Sanctuary and Repository visits do not count toward the three combats.
Ward examples. Cinder Sigil burns an entering Demon; Chains of Penitence stops movement; Bell of Return pushes a Demon backward; Martyr’s Lantern restores Altar Protection after a defeat. Watch the colored Ward artwork over the protected room.
Laws of Damnation — lasting rule changes. Choose one of three Laws at the start of a new run, then choose a new Law after each Boss. You can have up to three active Laws; choosing another replaces the oldest. Laws can help and hurt: Blood Is Power spends Blood to strengthen Basic Attacks, while Forbidden Scripture makes Empowered cards cheaper but regular cards more costly. Your active Laws appear in the run status area.

Crucifix Cards & Demon Merging

Hidden Crucifix cards. Approximately 15% of a fresh Demon deck secretly carry a Crucifix trigger. Defeat a marked Demon or let it reach the Altar to reveal one random Crucifix card. Some help, some harm, and some present choices. A hidden mark on a Demon left behind at passage end transfers to a future spawned Demon rather than disappearing.
Linked Demons — Blood Bonds. A newly entering Demon has a 15% chance, when an eligible partner exists, to link with a different Demon type on the board. Both display a LINK chain symbol and gain +1 Attack. Defeat either one, and the surviving partner enters persistent Blood Bond Rage: +2 Attack and 20% bonus maximum and current HP (rounded up). If one reaches the Altar, the bond breaks without granting Rage. This Blood Bond Rage is separate from temporary Priest-kill Enrage.
Demons also merge. Two matching Lesser Demons on the board merge into a Greater Demon that reappears in the Gateway. On Elite passages, matching Greater Demons may merge into an Ultra form with extra HP. Ward effects, Consecration, and certain Relics can interrupt merges.

Status Effects & Combat

Fire / Burning (Cinderbrand Relic). A Basic Attack ignites the target and schedules Fire damage at the end of the attacking Priest’s turn: half the current base Basic Attack damage, rounded down (minimum 1). A flame icon or fiery overlay indicates pending Fire damage. Multiple hits can queue additional burns.
Poison (Serpent’s Fang Relic). A Poison Basic Attack marks the target (1/2). A second Poison Basic Attack on that same target deals extra damage equal to the current Basic Attack damage and resets its count. Poison and Fire can appear together.
Lightning (Lightning Rod Relic). Basic Attack deals normal damage to the chosen Demon, then arcs to every other Demon in its room for half your base Basic Attack damage, rounded up. No room-wide splash when attacking a Boss. The lightning badge is a brief visual effect, not a lasting debuff.
Enrage / Rage. Each Demon killed during a Priest turn adds +1 HP and +1 Attack to surviving Demons on the next Priest turn. That bonus is temporary; without new kills, it fades on a later turn. Plan your killing blows to control the next wave.
Other effects. Stop / Bind prevents a Demon from advancing for its duration; Marks add damage to upcoming hits; Silence temporarily suppresses Demon abilities. Gatewalkers (gray gate symbol) can spawn directly in the Hall, Chapel, or Crypt instead of the Gateway.

Relics & Run Progression

Relic XP. Demon kills add Relic XP based on Blood rewards. XP-earned Relics cost 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, then 100 XP; the tenth costs 120 and each subsequent Relic increases the requirement by 20. Elite passage rewards grant extra Relic XP rather than a guaranteed Relic.
Long-term progression. Cinders bank after the third Boss and the Legacy checkpoint opens. The next 15-floor cycle continues the same run at 1.5× enemy difficulty. Within a cycle, Demon and Boss HP and damage also gain approximately 3% per floor.

Roguelite Run Guide

What Changed

15 Floors • 3 Acts. Floors 5, 10, and 15 are Boss floors. Your Altar, Blood, cards, Corruption, and Relics carry through the run.
Branching routes. After combat you choose among Standard Battles, Elite Battles, Hellgate Swarms, and non-combat Sanctuaries.
Relics are Blood XP rewards. Every Demon kill grants Relic XP equal to the Demon Blood earned from that kill. The first XP Relic costs 15 XP; each XP-earned Relic raises the next cost by 5.
Room reward Relics are bonus Relics. If you clear a room and choose a Relic reward, it does not increase the XP required for your next XP-earned Relic.
Build during the run. Rewards still include Priest-card drafts using the original card pool/art, recovery, Demon Blood, and bonus Relics.
Cinders bank every 3 Acts. Defeat the third Boss of a cycle to secure the Cinders you earned, open the Legacy shop, then continue the same run at double the previous cycle difficulty.
Health rises with depth. Enemy max HP gains +15% of base HP for every floor descended, stacking with the Ascension-cycle multiplier. Attack scaling remains tied to Ascension difficulty.
Original combat remains intact. Rooms, Demon movement, Corruption, Consecration, Ritual rolls, Empowering, Relic effects, and Boss abilities still use the original systems. Demon kills no longer draw Crucifix cards.
Visual target popups for Priest cards. Any Priest card that targets a Demon now opens the illustrated target popup, showing all current Demons on the board, and Boss illustrations whenever that card can legally target the Boss.
